We process your data based on:

6. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access: know what data we have about you.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten"): delete your account and data.
  • Objection: object to certain processing.
  • Restriction: temporarily limit use of your data.
  • Portability: receive your data in structured format.
  • Withdraw consent: at any time, without retroactive effects.

To exercise these rights: spothecliff@gmail.com (response within a maximum of 30 days).

You may also file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) if you believe your rights have been violated.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS)
  • Hashed passwords (never stored in plain text)
  • Restricted database access
  • Periodic backups

No system is 100% secure. If we detect a security breach affecting you, we will notify you within 72 hours as required by applicable regulations.

8. Minors

The App is prohibited for minors under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we detect a minor has registered, we will delete their account and data.

If you are a parent/guardian and believe a minor in your care has registered without permission, contact us to delete the account: spothecliff@gmail.com.

9. Cookies

The App does not use traditional web cookies. We only use technical identifiers necessary to maintain your session.

10. Changes to this Policy

We will update this Policy when necessary. Major changes will be notified within the App with reasonable notice. Continued use after a change implies acceptance.
